# src/Blazix.Primitives/Interop

The twins: the C# half of each JavaScript module (ADR 04 §3). Internal to the library (ADR 08 §1); parts talk to twins and never to `IJSRuntime`.

## Inputs

- `docs/decisions/04-js-interop-boundary.md` §3 (contract, decision points), ADR 01 (Server floor), ADR 06 §1 (twin contract tests), ADR 08 §3 (`<Module>Twin`), ADR 16 rule 4 (one `JsonSerializerContext`).
- `../js/twin.ts`, `../js/decide.ts`, `../js/event-info.ts`: the JavaScript side of the same contract.
- `../Events/EventSnapshot.cs` (Track 2): the event snapshot and its JSON names, which `../js/event-info.ts` mirrors.

## Job

- `TwinBase<TTwin, TOptions>`: lazy `import()` of the module on first use; `AttachAsync(ElementReference | string, options, TwinReferences? references = null)` returning a `TwinHandle<TOptions>` per attached part, idempotent per element, and `AttachTokenAsync(string token, options, TwinReferences? references = null)`, the token form of a part without an element (JavaScript receives `null` as the element; ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27), idempotent per token; the one `DotNetObjectReference<TTwin>` the module calls back through; `Ping` and `Decide` as `[JSInvokable]` methods; `DisposeAsync` that releases every handle, the reference and the module. A `JSDisconnectedException` (the Server circuit is gone) turns every call into a no-op rather than an exception in a part's lifecycle method.
- `TwinHandle<TOptions>`: `UpdateAsync(options, TwinReferences? references = null)` and an idempotent `DisposeAsync()`; `update` after `dispose` is a no-op.
- `OpenChangeRelay(int Revision, string Reason, EventKind EventKind, string EventType, string? PointerType, string? TriggerId, int? CoalescedCloseRevision = null, string? CoalescedCloseReason = null)` (ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27, the open-change relay; the record is track 1's, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-27): the last open change a popup committed, built by the popup store at commit with `OpenChangeRelay.From(revision, reason, snapshot, triggerId)` (the revision counter in the store's non-reactive context, the reason as its literal). The `interactions` and `focus-manager` options carry it as a nullable member (`Relay`), and each module emits its own `openchange` when `Revision` moves (FLT-EVT-02's details; the open state travels in each module's own options and `nested` is the module's, as settled with the interactions track's shared names, ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27). JSON: `{"revision":3,"reason":"trigger-press","eventKind":"PointerEvent","eventType":"click","pointerType":"mouse","triggerId":"base-ui-5"}`, `pointerType` and `triggerId` omitted when `null`. Tested by `OpenChangeRelayTests` (L1). An open committed after a close that no render-batch boundary has seen also carries that close's revision and reason (JSON `coalescedCloseRevision`, `coalescedCloseReason`, omitted while `null`; ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27, the relay's Server semantics), and the module emits the close's `openchange` first.
- `TwinReferences` (ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27, element-typed option values cross as interop arguments, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-27; M4 track 1): the elements (`Add(ElementReference)`) and script-held objects (`Add(IJSObjectReference)`, a closed shadow root say) one `attach` or `update` sends after its options, as one interop argument that Blazor revives; `Add` returns the index the options carry. An options record names an element-typed value by its index, in an `int?` field the module names (omitted from the JSON while `null`): `PortalOptions.ContainerRef`, positioning's `AnchorRef` and `BoundaryRefs` (track 2), the focus manager's element options (track 4); the module reads it with `resolveReference` from `js/twin.ts`. An empty or absent list sends nothing after the options, so `attach` keeps its three arguments and `update` its one. `TwinReferences.Add(ElementReference)` is the one boxing site of the array (BLZX2005 suppressed there with its reason). Tested by `TwinContractTests` and `twin.test.ts`.
- `TwinOptions`: the base of every options record; `Style` is the consumer's merged style string, sent on `attach` and on every `update` (handover at attach).
- `DecisionPolicy` and its kebab-case literals (`run`, `stop`, `start`, `prevent-and-stop`).
- `ButtonTwin` (module `button`): `ButtonOptions` carries the mechanism's configuration (`Disabled`, `FocusableWhenDisabled` with `null` for unspecified, `Native`, `Composite` as the part resolved it, `PressAndHold` with the 400/60 ms and 8 px defaults, `ReportInteractionType`, `DevelopmentChecks`, which defaults to the development warnings switch, and four options sent only when set: `HiddenInput` (the hidden-input bridge of Switch, Checkbox and Radio: a root click is default-prevented and forwarded with its modifiers to the checkbox or radio input the part renders as its next sibling, past an `<input type="hidden">`, found by position at click time because it has no `id` under `NativeButton`; under a `RenderFunction` callback this assumes the callback renders one top-level element), `ReadOnly` (the bridge ignores root clicks and reverts input clicks) `SuppressEnter` (an Enter keydown is prevented and never activates; Checkbox and Radio) and `InputItem` (Toolbar.Input: no key activates the element and its Space is typed; the disabled, focusable key prevention and press suppression stay)). `ActivationDecider` is the C# chain of the `activate` decision point: return `Stop` when a consumer handler called `PreventPartHandler()`. `ToggleDecider`: the C# request of the `toggle` decision point, asked inside the bridged input's click on WebAssembly; `Stop` reverts the box natively (ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-22, chosen by the user 2026-10-22). Events: `Toggled` (`ButtonToggle`: the input's new `checked` and the click it received, when the toggle was not decided synchronously, which is always on Server), `InteractionReported` (the literal `mouse`, `touch`, `pen`, `keyboard` or empty, with the event), `HoldTicked` (the event that started the sequence, every tick), `HoldStopped` (the release), `HoldClicked` (a click that performs the single action), `NativeButtonMismatched` (the declared mode). The part renders the button attributes itself; the module writes none.
- `DomTwin` (module `dom`): `DomOptions` turns primitives on per element: `Checked` (the hidden checkbox or radio input's property, set on attach and on every update, never inside a native click) with `CheckedRestores` (a count the part raises after a request that left its value different from a native click's, so an unchanged value still sends an update; ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-22), `Indeterminate` (the checkbox input's property, re-asserted inside a native click), `LiveRegion` (the announcement marker), `Image` (`ImageLoadOptions`: the source properties, `KeepMounted` to derive from the attached `<img>` instead of a probe, `HideWhenLoaded` for the fallbacks' ids, separated by spaces). Event: `ImageStatusChanged` with `loading`, `loaded` or `error`, once per change. The module's measuring and observing helpers have no twin: other modules import them.
- `TransitionsTwin` (module `transitions`).
  - `TransitionsOptions` holds:
    - the machine input `Open`;
    - the values the element was rendered with, read on `attach` only: `Mounted`, and `Status` as a `TransitionStatus?` travelling as the literal `starting`, `ending`, `idle` or nothing;
    - the machine options `EnableIdleState` and `DeferEndingState`, fixed per part;
    - `KeepMounted`, `HiddenUntilFound`, `HiddenWhenUnmounted` (a container is hidden at exit end; an indicator is not), `SuppressEndingStyle` (a kept element plays no exit), `ReportOpenComplete`, `RevealOnBeforeMatch`, `UnmountRequests` (a count C# raises to end an exit itself), and `ExternalExit` (no exit watcher: the exit ends only when `UnmountRequests` increases; the Collapsible Root and Trigger, whose exit ends with the panel's);
    - `OpenSelector`, the selector the element matches while rendered open (`[data-open]`, `[data-panel-open]`, `:not([aria-hidden])`): when set, the module reads the open input from the DOM after every render, before paint;
    - `Style`, applied only when set.
  - There is no `AnimateInitialOpen` field: C# renders the initial status and sends it as `Status`.
  - Events, each raised once per message:
    - `StatusChanged` (`TransitionStatusChange(TransitionStatus? Status, bool Mounted)`);
    - `TransitionCompleted` (`false` for an exit, after which the element is unmounted and its status unset; `true` for an enter).
  - The reveal:
    - `RevealDecider` (`Func<EventSnapshot, DecisionPolicy>`) is the C# chain of the decision point `reveal`: it requests the open and returns `Stop` when the request was canceled. It runs on WebAssembly only, inside the dispatch.
    - Elsewhere the module applies `run` and invokes `OnRevealRequest`, which returns `Task<bool>`: the answer of `RevealRequestHandler` (`Func<EventSnapshot, ValueTask<bool>>`), whether the part is open once the request ran. The module re-collapses the element a frame after a `false` answer.
- `CollapsibleTwin` (module `collapsible`, the Collapsible and Accordion panel's component module).
  - `CollapsibleOptions`: `Open`; `Mounted` and `Status` as the element was rendered (read when the module starts on an element); `KeepMounted`, `HiddenUntilFound`; `SuppressOpenAnimation` (the one-shot `animation-name: none` of an initially open or re-attached panel, read when the module starts on an element); `PanelId` (by which a withheld or replaced element is found again); `OpenSelector` (`[data-open]`); `Style`, the consumer's merged string, which the module composes the whole `style` from. `HeightVariable` and `WidthVariable` (the names an Accordion panel writes its measured size to, `--accordion-panel-height/width`; `null` and omitted for Collapsible's; ADR 04 note dated 2026-10-26). `ReportStatus` (`null` and omitted unless set): the module then reports `OnPanelStatusChange` (`PanelStatusChange`), the machine's status before the forced idle, once per change, never the unset status nor an unmounted panel's `starting`; `CollapsibleTwin.StatusChanged` feeds `CollapsibleRootContext.MirrorStatus` for an Accordion Item, which has no root twin; the opt-in is `CollapsibleRootContext.PanelReportsStatus` (D1 and D5, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-26).
  - Events, each once per message: `ForcedIdleChanged` (`PanelForcedIdleChange`: the panel shows itself settled during a motion-skipping open), `ExitCompleted` (`OnTransitionComplete` with `open: false`: the exit ended, the module has hidden the element), `AnimationTypeChanged` (`PanelAnimationTypeChange(Type, Mixed)`).
  - The reveal is `TransitionsTwin`'s: `RevealDecider` on WebAssembly, `OnRevealRequest` answered by `RevealRequestHandler` elsewhere.
- `CompositeTwin` (module `composite`, the roving group of one root element).
  - `CompositeOptions`: the root's configuration, `Orientation` (the literal `horizontal`, `vertical` or `both`, the default), `LoopFocus` (`true`), `EnableHomeAndEndKeys`, `StopEventPropagation` (`true`), `HighlightItemOnHover`, `ModifierKeys` (a subset of `Shift`, `Control`, `Alt`, `Meta`; empty), `DisabledIndices` (`null` reads the disabled state from the document), `Direction` (`ltr` or `rtl`), `Items` (`CompositeItemRegistration(Id, TabIndex, Disabled = false, FocusableWhenDisabled = false)`: the element id the module resolves, the consumer's `tabindex`, which the module never writes over, and the item's flags, sent only when set), `SelectOnNavigation` (the pending-navigation flag and the focus-driven selection of a radio group), `ReportFocusLeave`, `ReportHighlight`. `Style` stays unset: the module owns no style.
  - The M3 options of track 1 (ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-26, chosen by the user), each sent only when set:
    - `DeriveDisabledIndices`: the module derives the skipped indices from the flags (Toolbar).
    - `ReportItems`: the module reports the items' order (Tabs, and Toolbar if it needs it).
    - `RegistrationOnly`: a root-less list that only orders and reports its items, read once at `attach` (TabsRoot's panels, AccordionRoot's items).
    - `FollowActiveItem`: the highlight follows the item marked `data-composite-item-active`, unless it is `aria-disabled="true"` (TabsList).
  - Events, each once per change: `HighlightChanged` (the index, from `CompositeHighlightChange`), `FocusLeft` (the `focusout` snapshot, from `CompositeFocusLeave`), `KeyHandled` (option (i) of ADR 04's note of 2026-10-22, chosen by the user 2026-10-23) (the `keydown` the module handled and stopped without running the C# chain, from `CompositeKeyHandled`, only with `ReportHandledKeys`), and `ItemsChanged` (M3 track 1: the items' ids in index order, from `CompositeItemsChange(IReadOnlyList<string> Ids)`, with `ReportItems` or `RegistrationOnly`, once per publication that changed them).
  - Decision points, both with fallback `run`: `NavigatePoint` (`navigate`, before the module handles a key) answered by `NavigationDecider`, and `SelectPoint` (`select`, before it clicks a focused item's input) answered by `SelectionDecider`. The names were chosen by the user 2026-10-22.
  - From `attach` the module writes every item's `tabindex`; C# renders `-1` and never another value. The behaviours that drive the twin are in `../Composite/`.
- `FormTwin` (module `form`, the field and form infrastructure's mechanism module; the field half from M2 track 1).
  - `FormOptions` has one nullable record per attaching role. `Control` (`FormControlOptions`) carries `FormId`, `CustomValidity` (the field's own message, `null` to withdraw it), `Controlled`, `Value`, `DefaultValue`, `Revision` (an increase asks for a `sync` report), `ReportKeys`, `Disabled`, `ReportEvents`, `FocusTarget`, `AutoFocus`, `EnterSubmit` (a box's root: Enter submits the hidden input's form) and `LabelFallback` (a hidden input: look for its native label and report the label's id in a `label` report's `Value`); the last two are M2 track 5's, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-23 (ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-23). `Label` (`FormLabelOptions`) carries `Native`, `ControlId`, `Decides` and `DevelopmentChecks`. Track 2 adds `Submit`.
  - Events, each once per message: `ControlReported` (`FormControlReport`: `Type` `input`/`blur`/`keydown`/`enter`/`sync`/`focus`, `Value`, `Validity` (`NativeValidity`: the eleven flags, `ValidationMessage`, `WillValidate`, `Eligible`, `OwnMessageInstalled`), `Event`, `SameForm`, `Revision`) and `LabelMismatched` (`LabelMismatch(ExpectedNative)`).
  - The decision point `label` (`LabelPoint`) runs `LabelDecider` on WebAssembly; elsewhere the module applies `run`.
  - The submit half (M2 track 2): `Submit` (`FormSubmitOptions`: `Token`, the form's never-rendered token its controls carry as `FormId`; `FocusRevision`, `Focus`) for a form element. The invocation `OnSubmitRequest` (`FormSubmitRequest`: the submit snapshot, `SubmitterId`, a `FormControlState` per enrolled control) returns the JSON of a `FormSubmitAnswer` (`submit`, `blocked` with `Focus`, or `handled`), produced by `SubmitHandler` (`Func<FormSubmitRequest, Task<FormSubmitAnswer>>`).
- `PortalTwin` (module `portal`; M4 track 1, ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27, decision 5): `PortalOptions { ContainerRef, ContainerSelector, Lite }` plus `TwinOptions.Style` (JSON `{"containerRef":0,"lite":false,"style":"…"}`), attached to the portal node; the container crosses as index 0 of the references array (an `ElementReference`, a script-held `IJSObjectReference`, or the enclosing portal's node) or as a selector. No report and no decision point. `../Popup/PopupPortalBehavior` composes it for the portals: the wrapper's `display: contents`, the node's frozen `display: none` style, the outside guards and the owner (`BuildBeforeElement`/`BuildAfterElement`, while `PopupPortalContext.Publication` is non-modal and open, never lite), and `OnAfterRenderAsync(node, container)`, which attaches after the render that created the node, updates on a change of container or style, detaches when no node renders (a pending container) and completes `PopupPortalContext.Moved`.
- `ScrollLockTwin` (module `scroll-lock`; M4 track 1, ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27): `ScrollLockOptions { Enabled }` (JSON `{"enabled":true}`), attached to the popup's reference element, whose document is locked; no report and no decision point. `../Popup/PopupScrollLockBehavior` composes it for the popup families (Dialog's `open && modal === true`, Popover's anchored decision, tracks 8 and 5): `ApplyAsync(referenceElement, enabled)` from the part's `OnAfterRenderAsync` attaches once enabled and sends `Enabled` only on change. `Enabled` is `update`-carried, so on Server the lock applies a round trip after the commit (UTL-DOM-12's dated note).
- `TabsTwin` (module `tabs`, the Tabs family's component module; one handle on the List, one per Indicator).
  - `TabsOptions`: `Part` (`list` or `indicator`). For the List, `Orientation`, `ActivateOnFocus`, `Controlled` (fixed at the root's first parameter set), and `ReportClicks` and `ReportFocus`, set only while a Tab has a consumer `onclick` or `onfocus`. For an Indicator, `Hidden` (a consumer's `hidden`, which the module never writes over) and `TwinOptions.Style`.
  - Events, each once per message: `SelectionReported` (`TabsSelection(TabId, Direction, Event)`: an activation, on Server), `TabEventReported` (`TabsTabEvent(TabId, Event)`: a click or focus that activated nothing, only while reported), `DirectionChanged` (`TabsDirectionChange`: the direction the module wrote, once per change, for the parts' mirror) and `MeasurementChanged` (`TabsIndicatorMeasurement(Position, Size)`: an Indicator's measurement, once per change, both `null` while hidden).
  - The decision point `select` (`SelectPoint`, fallback `run`) is answered by `SelectionDecider` on WebAssembly, with the activation the module handed over just before through the synchronous invocation `OnSelecting` (`PendingSelection`; design point 6 of the Tabs track, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-26).
  - From `attach` the module writes `data-activation-direction` on the Root, the List, the Tabs, the Panels and the Indicators, and each Indicator's `hidden` and style; C# renders the first values under the handover. The parts that drive the twin are in `../Tabs/`.
- `PopupTwin` (module `popup`; M4 track 5, [issue 125](https://github.com/JakeMurrayDev/Blazix.Primitives/issues/125), ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27, the `popup` row; design point 5 of track 5, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-28): `PopupOptions { SessionToken, Viewport, Positioner, Popup }`, one role per handle. `PopupViewportOptions { Open, PopupId, ContentVersion }` on the Viewport element (JSON `{"viewport":{"open":true,"popupId":"…","contentVersion":2}}`; `ContentVersion` is the pull request's addition, Base UI re-measuring on every payload change); `PopupPositionerOptions { WatchTriggerChange, Cutout, ScrollLock, TouchOpen, ActiveTriggerId }` on the Positioner; `PopupElementOptions { StopCompositeKeys }` on the Popup. Reports: `OnViewportChange` → `ViewportChanged(PopupViewportReport(string? ActivationDirection, bool Transitioning))`, `OnTriggerChangeSettled` → `TriggerChangeSettled`. No decision point yet (the trigger guards' `guard` follows with the focus manager). Composed by Popover's Viewport, Positioner and Popup; Tooltip's and PreviewCard's Viewports attach the same Viewport role. `PopupModuleAttachment` (2026-10-28, moved from `../Popover/` for the popup families' parts, settled by the orchestrator) attaches one part's handle after the render that created its element, awaits a pending first attach, and sends an options record only when it changed.
- `PositioningTwin` (module `positioning`, floating positioning's mechanism module; one handle per Positioner, its Arrow has none; M4 track 2).
  - `PositioningOptions`: `SessionToken` (the module takes the `interactions` session of this token at attach, and a new one on a change); `OpenSelector` (`null` for `[data-open]`; mounted is the absence of `hidden`, both read from the applied batch); `KeepMounted`; `TransitionStatus` (`starting` adds `transition: none`); `TriggerId`; `AnchorId`, `AnchorRef` (the anchor element's index in the references) and `VirtualAnchor` (`PositioningRect(X, Y, Width, Height)`); `PositionMethod`, `Side`, `Align` (literals, `absolute`, `bottom`, `center` by default); `SideOffset` and `AlignOffset` (numbers), or `SideOffsetFunction` and `AlignOffsetFunction` (`PositioningOffset(Constant, AnchorWidth, AnchorHeight, PositionerWidth, PositionerHeight, Overrides)` with `PositioningOffsetOverride(Side, Align, …the same terms)`, the list compared item by item); `CollisionBoundary` (`clipping-ancestors`), `BoundaryRect` or `BoundaryRefs` (`PositioningIndexes(Start, Count)`, the boundary elements' consecutive indexes in the references, compared by value and serialized as a JSON array); `CollisionPadding` (a number) or `CollisionPaddingSides` (`PositioningPadding`); `Sticky`; `ArrowPadding` (`5`); `DisableAnchorTracking`; `CollisionAvoidance` (`PositioningCollisionAvoidance(Side, Align, FallbackAxisSide)` literals); the private `Shift` (`PositioningShift(CrossAxis, RootBoundary)`) and `LazyFlip` (`side` or `placement`); `AdaptiveOrigin`; `Direction`; `ArrowId` and `ArrowStyle`; `TwinOptions.Style`. A record with value equality, so an equal re-render sends nothing. The anchor and boundary elements cross as `TwinReferences` after the options, the anchor first.
  - Events, each once per change of the mirrored values, never per pass: `PlacementChanged` (`PositioningPlacement(Side, Align, AnchorHidden, Uncentered)`).
  - No decision point: every point answers `run`. From `attach` the module writes the Positioner's `style`, `data-side`, `data-align` and `data-anchor-hidden`, and the Arrow's `style`, `data-side`, `data-align` and `data-uncentered`; C# renders the first values under the handover. The behaviours that drive the twin are in `../Floating/`.
- `FocusManagerTwin` (module `focus-manager`; one handle per focus-managed popup, on the Popup part's element).
  - `FocusManagerOptions`: `Modal` (default `true`), `Disabled`, `InitialFocus` (default `true`) with `InitialFocusElement` (the index of a reference passed with the options through `TwinReferences`) or `InitialFocusId`, `ReturnFocus` (default `true`; the parts' `FinalFocus`) with `ReturnFocusElement` or `ReturnFocusId`, `ExplicitReturnFocus` (`null`: explicit exactly when a target is set), `CloseInteractionType` (the close type C# classified), `RestoreFocus` (`FocusManagerTwin.RestoreNone`, `RestoreTabbable`, `RestorePopup`), `CloseOnFocusOut` (default `true`), `OpenInteractionType` (the literal, default `""`, `null` for a programmatic open and sent as `null`), `OpenSelector` (default `[data-open]`), `TreatPopupAsFloatingElement` (otherwise the popup element's parent is the floating element), `DomReferenceId`, `TriggerFocusGuards`, `PreviousFocusableElement` and `NextFocusableElement` (reference indices, Menu's), `InsideElements` (reference indices), `InsideElementIds`, `Relay` (the popup core's `OpenChangeRelay`, JSON `relay`, 2026-10-28) and `Answered` (raised by the behaviour for every request it routed, closed or canceled, and sent outside a render, so the module asks again after a cancel; ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-27, settled by the orchestrator). `SessionToken` (the popup's interactions session, which the module joins and then reads in place of `Relay`, 2026-10-28). Value equality compares the lists by item, so an unchanged render sends no `update`. Names: design point 8 of M4 track 4, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-27. The behaviour (`../Floating/FocusManagerBehavior.cs`) sends changed options from the commit path, before the batch (design point 1, chosen by the user 2026-10-27; ADR 01 Amendments 2026-10-27).
  - Events, each once per message: `FocusOutCloseRequested` (`FocusOutCloseRequest(Event, InteractionType)`, reason `EventReason.FocusOut`; once per open) and `InteractionTypeChanged` (the literal the module recorded, `""` after an open reset it). No decision point (`OnDecide` answers `Run`).
  - The module reads the open state from the applied batch, never from `update` after `attach`; it writes the outside marking, the `data-tabindex` stash, the managed `tabindex` and the inside guards' VoiceOver role (`../js/CONTEXT.md`).
- `TransitionStatusRules` (`Initial(open, enableIdleState, animateInitialOpen)` and `Evaluate(open, mounted, status, deferEndingState)`, both returning `(bool Mounted, TransitionStatus? Status)`): the C# half of the machine. It covers a part's first render and the render that applies a new open state. The `transitions` module runs the same evaluation step for an attached element; `js/test-support/transition-evaluation.cases.json` tests both.
- `TransitionStatus` (`public enum TransitionStatus { Starting, Ending, Idle }`, namespace `Blazix.Primitives`, file `TransitionStatus.cs` beside its first consumer): a part declares `[property: MirroredField] TransitionStatus? TransitionStatus`, `null` being the unset status.
- A part owns its twin: a callback carries no element key, so one twin attached to several elements cannot tell their reports apart. The twin's host element may be its Root's element (ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-21, issue 64): the preload `Avatar.Image` attaches its own `DomTwin` to the `Avatar.Root` element, because the `<img>` exists only once loaded, and keeps one live handle. A part's twin may also host an element the part renders beside its own (M2 track 4, settled by the orchestrator 2026-10-22 under ADR 04 Amendments 2026-10-21, the Root-element reading): `SwitchRoot` (and Checkbox and Radio after it) attaches a `DomTwin` to the hidden input it renders after its element, by the input's captured `ElementReference`, beside the `ButtonTwin` on its root; the attach message per twin is accepted. `dom` and `button` own no style property, so their options leave `Style` unset and the part keeps rendering its style; `transitions` applies `Style` only when a part hands its style over.
- `InteractionType` (`Mouse`, `Touch`, `Pen`, `Keyboard`, `Unknown` for the empty literal) arrived with M4 track 1 in `../Popup/InteractionType.cs` (ADR 08 Amendments 2026-10-27); `InteractionTypeLiterals.FromLiteral(string?)` and `ToLiteral(InteractionType)` are the one mapping from the literals the twins report (`ButtonInteraction.InteractionType` and the popup modules' reports), which keep passing literals. `TransitionStatus` arrived with its twin (track 5). `ImageLoadingStatus` arrived with Avatar (track 7, `../Avatar/ImageLoadingStatus.cs`); `DomTwin` keeps reporting the literals and `AvatarImage` maps them.
- `InteractionsTwin` (module `interactions`, M4 track 3, issue 119; one twin per attaching part: a popup root's session by token through `TwinBase.AttachTokenAsync`, a trigger, the floating element, a tooltip provider by token). `PressDecider`: the C# chain of `press`, run only over a press handed over by `OnPressing` (`PendingPress`), consumed after; `EscapeDecider`: the C# chain of `escape`, which runs the open-change request with `EventReason.EscapeKey` and answers `PreventAndStop`, `Prevent`, `Stop` or `Run` from `IsCanceled` and `IsPropagationAllowed` (`PreventAndStop` without one). Events: `RequestReported` (`InteractionsRequest(Open, Reason, Event, TriggerId, Instant)`), `PressReported` (`InteractionsPress(Open, Reason, TriggerId, InteractionType, Event)`, Server). `InteractionsLiterals.ParseReason` maps the module's reason literals.
